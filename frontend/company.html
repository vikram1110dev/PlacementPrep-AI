Preparation Roadmap & Rounds
Round 1: Online Assessment

90 minutes coding round usually on HackerEarth or similar platform. Contains 2 medium-to-hard coding questions.

Focus: Arrays, Trees, DP
Round 2: Technical Interview 1

45 minutes problem solving round. The interviewer will ask 1-2 DSA questions and expect you to write clean, optimal code.

Focus: Graphs, System Design (Basic)
Round 3: Technical Interview 2

Deep dive into your resume, projects, and advanced data structures. Often includes OS and DBMS questions.

Focus: Projects, Core CS Subjects
Round 4: Googliness / HR Round

Behavioral round focusing on cultural fit, leadership principles, and situational questions.

Focus: Soft Skills, Communication
